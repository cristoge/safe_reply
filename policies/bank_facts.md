# Bank facts

The facts of our fictional digital bank, in one place. Every canned answer
(`policies/canned/`) takes its numbers, times and app paths from here, so they
never contradict each other. In v2 this is also the base of the policy manual.

Where a real public rule exists (SEPA, PSD2, the EU Instant Payments
Regulation), we follow it. Everything else is a business decision of the
fictional bank.

Times we control are written as "usually": we only promise what a regulation
guarantees.

## General

- We are a digital bank: no branches, no cheques and no cash deposits. Everything
  happens in the app.
- Accounts are in euros (EUR), with an IBAN.
- Business days: Monday to Friday, except public holidays in the euro area
  payment calendar (TARGET).
- Support: Help › Contact us in the app. It shows our phone number and a chat.
- We will never ask for your PIN, passwords or one-time codes, by phone,
  email or chat.

## Account and personal details

- Minimum age to open an account: 18.
- Who can open an account: residents of a country of the European Economic Area
  (the 27 EU countries plus Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway), with a valid
  identity document and a mobile phone number.
- Personal details (address, email) can be changed in Profile › Personal
  details. A change of name needs a photo of a new identity document with the
  new name. A change of phone number needs a one-time code sent to the new
  number.

## Identity verification

- Why: EU anti-money laundering rules require us to verify the identity of every
  customer before they can use the account, and again when documents expire.
  It also protects customers: nobody can open an account in their name with a
  stolen document.
- How: in Profile › Verify identity, a photo of a valid identity document
  (passport, national identity card or residence permit) and a selfie.
- Most checks finish within minutes. Some need a manual review, which usually
  takes up to 2 business days.
- Common reasons a check fails: the document is expired, the photo is blurry or
  has glare, part of the document is cut off, or the selfie doesn't match.

## Cards: ordering and activation

- Our cards are Visa debit cards.
- Every account comes with a free virtual card, ready to use as soon as the
  account is open.
- Physical card: the first one is free. Order it in Cards › Order physical card.
- Delivery: usually 3 to 5 business days after ordering; up to 10 business days
  to some countries. Delivery is by standard post, with no tracking number.
- Activation: in Cards › Activate card, with the code printed on the letter that
  comes with the card. Contactless works after the first payment with chip and
  PIN.
- Spare card: you can have up to 2 physical cards at the same time, both on the
  same account and balance. The second one costs €5.
- Virtual cards: free, up to 5 at the same time, created instantly in Cards ›
  New virtual card. They work online and with Apple Pay and Google Pay, not in
  ATMs.
- Disposable virtual cards: the card number changes after every payment. Up to
  5 a day. They can't be used for subscriptions or recurring payments, or for
  payments that are held first and charged later (hotels, car hire, petrol
  stations).
- Apple Pay and Google Pay: supported. Add a card in Cards › Add to wallet.
- Expiry: cards are valid for 3 years. We send a new physical card automatically
  about 1 month before the old one expires, for free, if the account is active.
  The card number changes; the PIN stays the same.
- Linking: a new physical card is linked to your account when you activate it in
  the app. Cards from other banks can be saved in the app only to top up.

## Cards: usage problems

- PIN: you can see it at any time in Cards › Show PIN, after confirming your
  identity in the app. You can change it at most ATMs that offer a PIN change
  (not available in every country).
- After 3 wrong PIN attempts, the PIN is blocked. Unblock it in Cards › Unblock
  PIN; the next payment must be with chip and PIN.
- Contactless (EU rules, PSD2): up to €50 per payment. After 5 contactless
  payments in a row, or €150 in total since the last time you used your PIN, the
  card asks for the PIN once.
- Common reasons a card payment is declined: not enough balance, the card is
  frozen, a wrong PIN, online payments or contactless turned off in Cards ›
  Security, the expiry date or security code typed wrong, or the payment not
  confirmed in the app (3-D Secure).
- Acceptance: the card works wherever Visa is accepted. Some merchants don't
  accept debit cards for payments that are held first (some hotels, car hire,
  petrol stations and toll roads).
- Pending card payments: the merchant has up to 7 days to complete the payment.
  If they don't, the amount is released, usually within 7 days; for hotels and
  car hire it can take up to 30 days.
- Card payment fees: we don't charge for card payments in euros. Payments in
  another currency use our exchange rate (see Currency exchange). Some merchants
  add their own fee; that fee is theirs, not ours.
- Card retained by an ATM: freeze it in Cards › Freeze card and order a new one
  in Cards › Replace card. The replacement is free.

## ATMs and cash

- The card works in any ATM with the Visa logo. We have no ATMs of our own.
- Withdrawals are free up to €200 a calendar month. After that, we charge 2% of
  the amount (minimum €1).
- The ATM owner may charge their own fee. The ATM shows it before you confirm.
- Daily withdrawal limit: €1,000. Each ATM may have a lower limit of its own.
- Pending withdrawals: usually completed within 3 business days. If the ATM
  didn't give you the cash, the amount is released automatically, usually
  within 7 business days.

## Transfers

- SEPA transfers (euros, within the SEPA area): free.
  - Standard: reach the recipient's bank by the end of the next business day
    (EU rule, PSD2). Sent after 16:00 CET or on a non-business day, they count
    from the next business day.
  - Instant: reach the recipient's bank within 10 seconds, at any time, if the
    recipient's bank offers it (EU Instant Payments Regulation). Same price as a
    standard transfer: free.
- Transfers outside SEPA, or in other currencies (SWIFT): €5 per transfer, plus
  our exchange rate if we convert. Usually 1 to 5 business days. Banks in the
  middle and the recipient's bank may deduct their own fees.
- Name check (Verification of Payee): before a euro transfer we check that the
  recipient's name matches the IBAN, and show you the result. If it doesn't
  match, think twice: it is a common sign of fraud.
- Cancelling: an instant transfer can't be cancelled. A standard transfer can't
  be cancelled once it has been sent. A scheduled transfer can be cancelled in
  Payments › Scheduled until the day before it is due.
- Common reasons a transfer is declined: not enough balance, a wrong IBAN, the
  name check failed and wasn't confirmed, the amount is above your limit, or the
  recipient's bank or country is not supported.
- Recipients we can't send to: countries under EU sanctions and some
  high-risk countries. The app says so when you add the recipient.
- Receiving money: share your IBAN from Account › Account details. Incoming SEPA
  transfers are free and usually show in your balance on the day they arrive.
  An incoming standard SEPA transfer can take until the end of the next business
  day after it was sent.

## Top-ups

- Ways to top up (Account › Top up): with a debit or credit card, by bank
  transfer to your IBAN, or with Apple Pay or Google Pay.
- No cash and no cheques: we have no branches.
- Card top-ups: instant. Free with Visa and Mastercard cards issued in the
  European Economic Area; 2% with cards issued outside it or business cards.
  American Express is not accepted.
- Bank transfer top-ups: free. Same times as receiving a SEPA transfer.
- Card top-up limits: at least €10 per top-up, up to €3,000 per top-up and
  €10,000 per 30 days. They also apply to automatic top-ups and to top-ups with
  Apple Pay or Google Pay. Bank transfer top-ups have no limit of ours.
- Automatic top-up (Account › Auto top-up): when your balance goes below an
  amount you choose, we top up the amount you choose (at least €10) from your
  saved card.
- Verifying a new card: the first time you top up with a card, we may make a
  small temporary charge with a 6-digit code in its description, so we know the
  card is yours. Enter the code in the app to verify the card. The charge is
  released, usually within 7 days.
- A card top-up can be pending when the card's bank asks for extra checks;
  usually completed within 2 business days.
- A top-up can be reverted when the card's bank declines it after approving it,
  or when the card can't be verified. The money goes back to your card, usually
  within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card's bank.

## Currency exchange

- Exchange in Account › Exchange. You can hold and exchange 10 currencies: EUR,
  USD, GBP, CHF, PLN, SEK, DKK, NOK, CZK and HUF.
- Rate: the market rate at the moment you exchange, plus 0.5% Monday to Friday
  and 1% on Saturdays and Sundays (markets are closed). The app shows the rate
  and the fee before you confirm.
- Card payments and withdrawals in another currency use the same rate and fee,
  at the moment the payment is processed. If you hold that currency, we use it
  first and there is no exchange.
- No cryptocurrencies.

## Unauthorised payments (always handled by a person)

- Report a payment you didn't make as soon as possible, and within 13 months
  (EU rule, PSD2). Freeze the card first in Cards › Freeze card.
- If the payment was unauthorised, we refund it by the end of the next business
  day after it is reported, unless we have reasons to suspect fraud by the
  customer. The customer's liability before reporting is at most €50, unless
  they acted with gross negligence or fraud.
