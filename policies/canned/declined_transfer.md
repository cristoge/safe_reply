The most common reasons a transfer is declined are:
- There isn't enough balance for the transfer and its fees.
- The IBAN is wrong.
- The name check failed (the recipient's name didn't match the IBAN) and you didn't confirm it.
- The amount is above your limit.
- The recipient's bank or country is not supported.
