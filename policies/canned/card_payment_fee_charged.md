We don't charge for card payments in euros.

If you see a fee, the most common reasons are:
- The payment was in another currency: we use the market rate plus 0.5% Monday to Friday, and 1% on Saturdays and Sundays. If you hold that currency, we use it first and there is no exchange.
- The merchant added their own fee: that fee is theirs, not ours.
- It was a cash withdrawal: free up to €200 a calendar month, then 2% (minimum €1), plus any fee of the ATM owner.
