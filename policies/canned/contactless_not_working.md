The most common reasons contactless doesn't work are:
- The card is new: contactless works after the first payment with chip and PIN.
- The payment is over €50: EU rules (PSD2) limit contactless to €50 per payment.
- After 5 contactless payments in a row, or €150 in total since you last used your PIN, the card asks for the PIN once. Pay once with chip and PIN and contactless works again.
- Contactless is turned off in Cards › Security.
- The card is frozen: check Cards › Freeze card.
