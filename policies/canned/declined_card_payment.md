The most common reasons a card payment is declined are:
- There isn't enough balance for the payment.
- The card is frozen: check Cards › Freeze card.
- The PIN was wrong (after 3 wrong attempts it is blocked: unblock it in Cards › Unblock PIN).
- Online payments or contactless are turned off in Cards › Security.
- The expiry date or security code was typed wrong.
- The payment wasn't confirmed in the app (3-D Secure).

If none of these is your case, let us know (see below).
