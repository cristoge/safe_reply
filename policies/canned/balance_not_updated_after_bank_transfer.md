Incoming transfers take a little time:
- An instant transfer arrives within 10 seconds, if the sender's bank offers it.
- A standard transfer can take until the end of the next business day after it was sent. If it was sent after 16:00 CET or on a non-business day, it counts from the next business day.

Check that the sender used the IBAN in Account › Account details exactly. If the time has passed and the IBAN is right, let us know (see below).
