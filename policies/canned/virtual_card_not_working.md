The most common reasons a virtual card doesn't work are:
- The card is frozen: check Cards › Freeze card.
- Online payments are turned off in Cards › Security.
- The expiry date or security code was typed wrong.
- The payment wasn't confirmed in the app (3-D Secure).
- There isn't enough balance for the payment.
- Virtual cards don't work in ATMs, and disposable ones can't be used for subscriptions or for payments that are held first and charged later (hotels, car hire, petrol stations).
