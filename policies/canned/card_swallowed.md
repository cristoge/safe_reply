If an ATM has kept your card:
1. Freeze it in Cards › Freeze card, so nobody can use it.
2. Order a new one in Cards › Replace card. The replacement is free.

The new card usually arrives 3 to 5 business days later, and up to 10 business days to some countries. Until then, your virtual card works for online payments and with Apple Pay and Google Pay.
