Cards are valid for 3 years. About 1 month before your card expires, we send you a new physical card automatically and for free, as long as your account is active. You don't need to order it.

The new card has a new number but the same PIN. When it arrives, activate it in Cards › Activate card and update your card details wherever you have saved them (subscriptions, online shops).
