The most common reasons a card top-up fails are:
- The amount is below €10, above €3,000, or over €10,000 in the last 30 days.
- The card isn't a Visa or Mastercard (American Express is not accepted).
- Your card's bank declined it: check that card's balance and that it allows online payments.
- The card hasn't been verified yet: the first time, we may ask you to enter a 6-digit code from a small temporary charge.

You can also top up by bank transfer to the IBAN in Account › Account details, which has no limit of ours.
