SEPA transfers in euros, standard or instant, are free.

If you were charged for a transfer, the most common reasons are:
- It went outside SEPA or in another currency (SWIFT): €5 per transfer, plus our exchange rate if we converted the money.
- A bank in the middle or the recipient's bank deducted their own fee. Those fees are theirs, not ours, and they can make the recipient get less than you sent.
