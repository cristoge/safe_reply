A disposable virtual card gets a new card number after every payment, so its details are useless to anyone who copies them. Create one in Cards › New virtual card. You can create up to 5 a day.

Disposable cards can't be used for subscriptions or recurring payments, or for payments that are held first and charged later (hotels, car hire, petrol stations). For those, use a standard virtual card or your physical card.
