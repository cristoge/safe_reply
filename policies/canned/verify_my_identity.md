To verify your identity, open Profile › Verify identity and follow the steps: a photo of a valid identity document (passport, national identity card or residence permit) and a selfie.

Most checks finish within minutes. Some need a manual review, which usually takes up to 2 business days.

The most common reasons a check fails are:
- The document has expired.
- The photo is blurry or has glare.
- Part of the document is cut off.
- The selfie doesn't match the photo on the document.
