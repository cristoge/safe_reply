To order a physical card:
1. Open Cards › Order physical card.
2. Check your delivery address.
3. Confirm the order.

Your first physical card is free; a second one costs €5. It usually arrives 3 to 5 business days later, and up to 10 business days to some countries. When it arrives, activate it in Cards › Activate card.
