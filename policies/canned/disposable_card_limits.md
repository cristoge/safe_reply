With disposable virtual cards:
- You can create up to 5 a day, in Cards › New virtual card.
- The card number changes after every payment.
- They can't be used for subscriptions or recurring payments, or for payments that are held first and charged later (hotels, car hire, petrol stations).

For those payments, use a standard virtual card or your physical card.
