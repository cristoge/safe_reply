Card top-ups are usually instant. A card top-up can stay pending when your card's bank asks for extra checks; it is usually completed within 2 business days.

Top-ups by bank transfer take the same time as any SEPA transfer: an instant transfer arrives within 10 seconds, and a standard one by the end of the next business day after it was sent (counting from the next business day if it was sent after 16:00 CET or on a non-business day).

If your top-up is still pending after that, let us know (see below).
