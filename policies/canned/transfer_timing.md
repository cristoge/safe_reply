How long a transfer takes depends on its type:
- SEPA instant: reaches the recipient's bank within 10 seconds, at any time, if their bank offers it.
- SEPA standard: reaches the recipient's bank by the end of the next business day. If you send it after 16:00 CET or on a non-business day, it counts from the next business day.
- Outside SEPA or in other currencies (SWIFT): usually 1 to 5 business days.

Business days are Monday to Friday, except public holidays in the euro area payment calendar.
