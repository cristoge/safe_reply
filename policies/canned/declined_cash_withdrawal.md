The most common reasons a cash withdrawal is declined are:
- There isn't enough balance for the withdrawal and its fees.
- You've reached the daily limit of €1,000, or the ATM's own limit, which can be lower.
- The PIN was wrong (after 3 wrong attempts it is blocked: unblock it in Cards › Unblock PIN).
- ATM withdrawals are turned off in Cards › Security.
- The card is frozen: check Cards › Freeze card.
- You used a virtual card: they don't work in ATMs.
