The most common reasons a card stops working are:
- The card is frozen: check Cards › Freeze card.
- A new physical card hasn't been activated yet: activate it in Cards › Activate card.
- The PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts: unblock it in Cards › Unblock PIN.
- Online payments, contactless or ATM withdrawals are turned off in Cards › Security.
- There isn't enough balance for the payment.
- The card has expired: we send a new one about 1 month before, which needs to be activated.

If it still doesn't work, freeze the card in Cards › Freeze card and let us know (see below).
