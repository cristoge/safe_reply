Physical cards usually arrive 3 to 5 business days after you order them, and up to 10 business days to some countries. They come by standard post, so there is no tracking number.

If you ordered yours more than 10 business days ago, check that your address in Profile › Personal details is right and let us know (see below).

While you wait, your virtual card works for online payments and with Apple Pay and Google Pay.
