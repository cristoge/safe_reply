To move money into your account, send a transfer to your IBAN, which you'll find in Account › Account details. SEPA transfers in euros are free on our side.

An instant transfer arrives within 10 seconds, if the sending bank offers it. A standard one arrives by the end of the next business day after it was sent.

You can also top up with a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay in Account › Top up.
