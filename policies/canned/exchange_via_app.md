To exchange money:
1. Open Account › Exchange.
2. Choose the currencies and the amount.
3. Check the rate and the fee, and confirm.

You can hold and exchange EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, PLN, SEK, DKK, NOK, CZK and HUF. The fee is 0.5% Monday to Friday and 1% on Saturdays and Sundays.
