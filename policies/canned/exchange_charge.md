When you exchange money, we use the market rate at that moment plus:
- 0.5% Monday to Friday.
- 1% on Saturdays and Sundays, when markets are closed.

The app shows the rate and the fee before you confirm. Card payments and withdrawals in another currency use the same rate and fee; if you hold that currency, we use it first and there is no exchange.
