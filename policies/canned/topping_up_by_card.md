To top up with a card:
1. Open Account › Top up and choose card.
2. Add a Visa or Mastercard debit or credit card (American Express is not accepted).
3. Enter the amount: from €10 to €3,000 per top-up, and up to €10,000 per 30 days.

Card top-ups are instant. They're free with cards issued in the European Economic Area, and cost 2% with cards issued outside it or business cards.

The first time you use a card, we may make a small temporary charge with a 6-digit code in its description; enter the code in the app to verify the card.
